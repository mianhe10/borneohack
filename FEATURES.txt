================================================================
WHATSAPP BOT
================================================================

--- ONBOARDING ---
- Consent collection (PDPA compliance)
- Country selection: Malaysia / Indonesia / Philippines
- State/province selection (auto-list per country)
- Business profile setup:
  * Owner name
  * Business name
  * Product/service description
  * Monthly revenue
  * Business age
  * Bank account status
  * SSM/registration status
- Language selection: Bahasa Malaysia / English

--- SALES RECORDING ---
- Natural language sale entry (type anything, AI extracts amount + item)
  e.g. "jual nasi lemak 3 bungkus rm15" or "sold 5 bags for rm50"
- Voice note recording → AI transcription → auto-processed as text
- Expense recording via natural language
  e.g. "beli bahan rm50" or "bought supplies rm80"
- AI smart intent detection — bot understands context without strict commands

--- CREDIT SCORING ---
- Deterministic AI credit score (0–100) based on 6 criteria:
  1. Transaction Consistency (25 pts)
  2. Revenue Strength vs stated income (20 pts)
  3. Business Age (15 pts)
  4. Formalization — SSM + Bank Account (20 pts)
  5. Record Volume — number of transactions (10 pts)
  6. Expense Discipline — expense records (10 pts)
- AI-generated score explanation + 3 improvement tips
- Score breakdown with visual bar chart (text-based)
- Score history tracking

--- LOAN FEATURES ---
- Loan readiness checklist (7 items, progress bar)
  * Country-specific loan programs:
    Malaysia: TEKUN/PUNB/BSN (up to RM50,000)
    Indonesia: KUR (up to Rp50,000,000)
    Philippines: SBCorp (up to ₱300,000)
- Loan referral letter generator (auto-formatted, ready to send to bank officer)
  * Requires credit score ≥ 70
- Credit certificate (official-looking, includes cert ID, stars, loan-ready status)

--- AI FEATURES ---
- AI business advisor chat (context-aware, knows your business profile)
- 30-day sales forecast with trend analysis + actionable tips + risk warning
  * Requires 3+ sales records
- Supply chain analysis (cost health, supplier tips, cost-saving strategies)
- ASEAN export guide (market assessment, step-by-step export plan, platforms, regulations)

--- CONTENT GENERATION ---
- AI-generated marketing content for 5 platforms:
  * Instagram caption + hashtags
  * WhatsApp broadcast message
  * TikTok video script
  * Facebook post
  * Promo/discount announcement
- All content tailored to business name, product, country, language

--- IMAGE VERIFICATION ---
- SSM certificate photo verification (AI reads cert, extracts details, fuzzy name match)
  * Auto-runs credit score after successful verification
- Bank document verification (bank statement / passbook / e-wallet)
  * Extracts account holder name, fuzzy match against profile
- Auto-detects image type (SSM cert / payment receipt / other)

--- PROFILE & DATA ---
- View full business profile
- View all stored data (PDPA data transparency)
- Credit certificate display
- Score breakdown by category
- Reset all data (GDPR/PDPA right to erasure)

--- GLOBAL COMMANDS (work from any state) ---
MENU / PROFIL / SIJIL / PINJAMAN / SUMMARY / DATA
BREAKDOWN / PECAHAN / RUJUK / REFER / KEMASKINI / UPDATE
BATAL / CANCEL / LANGKAU / SKIP / ENGLISH / BM / RESET

--- MULTI-COUNTRY SUPPORT ---
- Malaysia: RM currency, SSM registration, TEKUN/PUNB/BSN loans, state list
- Indonesia: Rp currency, NIB registration, KUR loans, province list
- Philippines: ₱ currency, DTI/SEC registration, SBCorp loans, region list

--- RELIABILITY FEATURES ---
- Message deduplication (prevents double-processing from WhatsApp re-delivery)
- Per-phone message queue (prevents race conditions from rapid messages)
- Gemini API: 20s timeout, 2x retry with backoff
- Meta API: 3x retry with exponential backoff, skips retry on auth errors

PWA DASHBOARD (http://localhost:3000)
================================================================

--- LOGIN ---
- MSME login: enter WhatsApp number → loads personal dashboard
- Bank login: access code "bank2026" → loads portfolio view

--- MOBILE VIEW (MSME Dashboard) ---
- Credit score circle (animated arc, 0–100)
- Score badge (Excellent / Good / Moderate / Low)
- Business name + owner display
- 4 stat cards: Total Revenue, Transactions, Avg per Sale, Best Sale
- Trend indicators per stat
- Loan pre-qualification CTA card (appears when score ≥ 70)
- Score breakdown bars (6 categories, visual progress bars)
- Score history chart (Chart.js line graph)
- Sales activity chart (7 days / 30 days / All time toggle)
- Loan readiness roadmap (7-item checklist with progress bar)
- Recent sales list (latest transactions)
- Credit certificate card (score, stars, status, cert ID)
- Share credit score via WhatsApp button

--- BANK/PORTFOLIO VIEW (Desktop) ---
- Portfolio summary stats: Total MSMEs, Loan Ready (70+), Avg Credit Score, Total Revenue
- Currency toggle: RM / Rp / ₱
- MSME portfolio table (sortable, searchable, filterable by status)
  * Pagination (10/25/50/100 per page)
  * Filter: All / Loan Ready / In Progress / Building
  * Search by name or business
- Score distribution bars
- Portfolio breakdown pie chart (Chart.js)
- Revenue trends line chart
- Loan pipeline view
- ASEAN map (canvas-drawn) with color-coded user dots per state/region
  * Green = Loan Ready (70+), Yellow = In Progress (50-69), Red = Building (<50)
  * Hover tooltip with user details
- Impact metrics (MSMEs onboarded, total transactions, loan-eligible, countries)
- Financial inclusion progress bars per country
- Country breakdown list
- Export report button (PDF)

--- MSME DETAIL MODAL ---
- Click any row in portfolio table → modal with full MSME profile
- Shows: name, business, product, credit score, total revenue, transactions, loan status
- Recent sales list
- Phone number row

--- PRE-QUALIFICATION MODAL ---
- For MSMEs with score ≥ 70
- Summary: credit score, revenue, transactions, verified sales
- Lists data being shared with bank
- "Send to Partner Bank via WhatsApp" button
- PDPA compliance note

--- PWA FEATURES ---
- Installable on Android/iOS (Add to Home Screen)
- Service worker (offline support for static assets)
- Install prompt banner
- New version update banner (auto-detects SW update)
- Pull-to-refresh (mobile)
- Dark/light theme toggle
- Language toggle (EN/BM)
- Navigation drawer (settings, refresh, theme, language, logout)
- Last updated timestamp
- Responsive: mobile-first, desktop portfolio view
